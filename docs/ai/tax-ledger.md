# Tax ledger (deferred debt)

"Tax" is work we knowingly left undone or did the quick way. Recording it makes the choice visible and owned. The owner rules on each entry.

Add a row when you consciously defer something. Do not log bugs found by accident (those are backlog items).
Numbers `T-N` are minted by the orchestrator only.

| Tax | Cost of leaving it | Paid by (name + date) | Trigger to revisit | Ref |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-1: (example) Skipped input validation on the import form | A bad file can fail a whole batch | the owner, 2026-11-11 | Before the first external user, or the first bad batch | #NNN, D-NNN |

Paid by = the named person who accepted the cost, and the date. No name and date means the row is not valid.

## Not tax
These do NOT belong here:
- Bugs nobody chose to leave (backlog)
- Ideas and wish-list items (backlog)
- Facts about behaviour (findings)
- Work in progress (board)
- Anything with no cost of leaving it
