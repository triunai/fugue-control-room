1. **HIGH — Refresh rotation can escape family revocation.** [RefreshTokenStore.cs:100](src/Fugue.Infrastructure/Identity/RefreshTokenStore.cs:100)  
   Rotation locks the presented token, but revocation has no family-wide serialization. **Reproduction:** rotate T0 into T1. Begin rotating T1, pausing after its conditional UPDATE but before inserting T2. Replay T0 or logout, allowing the family UPDATE to start and block on T1. Complete rotation. Under PostgreSQL READ COMMITTED, revocation’s statement snapshot misses newly inserted T2; T2 remains usable after revocation completes. Serialize rotation and revocation using a shared family lock or persistent revoked-family state.

2. **MED — Concurrent override writes can silently lose a deny and falsify audit state.** [OverrideAdmin.cs:36](src/Fugue.Infrastructure/Identity/OverrideAdmin.cs:36)  
   Overrides have no concurrency token; EF writes only changed properties. **Reproduction:** begin with `IsAllowed=false`. Requests A and B both load it. A allows the permission and commits.
